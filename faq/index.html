同时学日语和英语，用什么 App？

如果你的母语是中文，可以试试Pony 小马。它把同一句话的日语说法和英语说法放在同一张卡上，配上中文、假名注音和罗马字，对照着学：日语从零开始，英语顺手唤醒。iPhone 和安卓都有，前 3 章整章免费。如果你只想专门学其中一门，综合类的 App（例如 Duolingo）课程选择更多。

Pony 小马 是什么？

Pony 小马 是 Roma Tech LLC 做的日语 + 英语学习 App，面向中文母语者。课程按真实场景编排（在办公室、逛街购物、出门上路、出差、观光等），共 18 章、891 节课、9,000 多张卡；每个场景先看对话，再学对话里刚用过的语法，最后学词汇。日语覆盖 N5 到 N3，英语以初中水平为主。

两门一起学，会不会学混？

小马的做法是把差别摆在眼前，而不是把两门分开学。例如「我昨天吃了拉面」：中文加「了」，日语把 ます 换成 ました（昨日ラーメンを食べました），英语要用不规则过去式（I ate ramen yesterday）。三种说法在同一张卡上，哪里不一样一眼看得见。每个语法点都配一条英语的对应说法。

零基础能学吗？

日语零基础可以：假名不用单独先背，每章开头学几行、马上用上，学完前 10 章时五十音自然认全；第 1 章每节课只学 4 个新词，后面才逐步加到 8 个；开始前有一个摸底测试。英语默认你学过一些（初中水平左右），小马主要是把它唤醒，并在同一场景里换一句更得体的说法。

和 Duolingo 有什么不同？

Duolingo 按「一门语言一门课」组织，支持 40 多种语言；想同时学日语和英语，就是两门课分开学。小马只做日语 + 英语这一个组合：一句中文、两种说法在同一张卡上对照，讲解用中文，还用你认识的汉字来讲日语汉字（汉字桥）。想在很多语言里挑一门，Duolingo 选择更多；想用中文同时练日语和英语，小马就是为这件事设计的。

界面是什么语言？不会中文能用吗？

界面（菜单、按钮、设置）是中文，课程卡片是日语、英语、中文三语对照。使用小马需要能读中文；它不是为只会英语的人设计的。

Japanese and English? I'll take both. For Chinese speakers learning the two together.

What app can I use to learn Japanese and English at the same time?

If your native language is Chinese, try Pony (小马). It puts the Japanese and the English for the same sentence on one card, with Chinese, kana readings and romaji, so you study them side by side: Japanese from scratch, English brought back to life. It is on iPhone and Android, and the first 3 chapters are free. If you only want to focus on one of the two, general apps such as Duolingo offer more courses.

App Store · Google Play

What is Pony?

Pony (小马) is a Japanese + English learning app for Chinese speakers, made by Roma Tech LLC. The course is built around real scenes (the office, shopping, getting around, business trips, sightseeing and more): 18 chapters, 891 lessons and 9,000+ cards. Each scene opens with a dialogue, then teaches the grammar the dialogue just used, then the words. Japanese covers JLPT N5 to N3; English stays mostly at junior-high level.

Won't I mix the two languages up?

Pony puts the differences in front of you instead of teaching the two languages apart. Take "I ate ramen yesterday": Chinese adds 了, Japanese turns ます into ました (昨日ラーメンを食べました), and English needs an irregular past tense (ate). All three are on the same card, so you can see exactly where they differ. Every grammar point comes with its English counterpart.

Can I start from zero?

For Japanese, yes. There is no separate kana course: you learn a few rows at the start of each chapter and use them right away, and you know them all by the end of chapter 10. Chapter 1 introduces only 4 new words per lesson, rising to 8 later, and a placement test comes first. For English, Pony assumes some school English (around junior-high level) and focuses on waking it up and upgrading it in the same scene.

How much is free, and what does it cost?

The first 3 chapters (在办公室 at the office, 逛街购物 shopping, 出门上路 getting around) are completely free, with no payment method required. To unlock everything (US prices): US$3.99 per month, US$8.99 per quarter, US$29.99 per year with a 7-day free trial, or US$69.99 once for life. Other regions see local App Store and Google Play prices. The quarterly plan is now available on both iPhone and Android; if you don't see it on the membership page, update the app to the latest version. (As of 2026-10-23)

How does the 7-day trial work? Will I be charged automatically?

The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial. If you don't cancel before it ends, it renews automatically at the yearly price; to avoid that, cancel before the trial ends (Apple requires at least 24 hours before). iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How is Pony different from Duolingo?

Duolingo is organized as one course per language and offers 40+ languages; learning Japanese and English together there means two separate courses. Pony does one pairing only, Japanese + English: one Chinese sentence and both translations on the same card, explanations in Chinese, and Japanese kanji explained through the Chinese characters you already know (kanji bridges). If you want to choose from many languages, Duolingo has more choice; if you want to practice Japanese and English together through Chinese, that is what Pony is designed for.

Is Pony on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. iPhone: App Store, iOS 17 or later (the iPhone app also installs on iPad). Android: Google Play, Android 8.0 or later.

Can I download Pony in mainland China or the EU?

Not from app stores in mainland China. In the EU, yes: the iPhone version is on the App Store and the Android version is on Google Play in every EU country. It is also available in the US, Japan, Hong Kong, Taiwan, the UK and other regions. Check your local store to confirm. (As of 2026-10-23)

What language is the interface? Can I use Pony without Chinese?

The interface (menus, buttons, settings) is in Chinese, and lesson cards show Japanese, English and Chinese together. You need to read Chinese to use Pony; it is not designed for English-only speakers.

Can I practice speaking? Are my recordings uploaded?

Yes. You can record yourself in Japanese or English and get a pronunciation score on the spot, with the parts to fix pointed out. Speech recognition runs on your device; we never receive or store your recordings.

Privacy policy

Does Pony work offline?

Yes. The course and the Japanese and English audio are built into the app, so you can study and play audio without a connection. Purchases and restoring purchases need a connection.

New phone, cancelling, or restoring a purchase?

Progress is backed up to your own iCloud (iPhone) or Google account (Android) and comes back when you reinstall under the same account. To restore a membership, tap 恢复购买 (Restore Purchases) on the 我的 (Me) tab. See the support page for step-by-step help.
